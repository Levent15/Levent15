# Cleat Models and Sizing for a Copa Mundial / Tiempo Legend 10 Elite Fan (as of Sept 2026)

Buyer: normal US men's 10.5. Copa Mundial in US 9.5 (a full size down). Tiempo Legend 10 Elite in US 10.5 (true to size). Wants a leather or classic touch and comfort, FG or FG/AG soleplate.

Method note: the egress proxy blocked WebFetch for every site tried (nike.com, adidas.com, footballboots.co.uk, footyheadlines.com, lockhartbootblog.com, soccerreviewsforyou.com, runrepeat.com, prodirectsport.com, about.nike.com). So every finding below comes from search-engine snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not from full-page reads. The URLs are the pages those snippets came from. Treat exact numbers (prices, mm measurements) as "reported by the snippet", and re-check them on the product page before buying. I could not read the RunRepeat lab measurements.

## Ranked shortlist and size to try (synthesis of the sections below)

### Takeaway
The Tiempo Legend line has ended. Nike replaced it in January 2026 with the **Tiempo Maestro** (Elite $250-260, Academy $85-90) and the **Tiempo Ligera** (Pro $150-160). Both reportedly fit true to size, so a buyer who is 10.5 in the Legend 10 Elite should start at **10.5**. adidas's current Copa is the **Copa Pure 4 (IV)**, launched in September 2025. It fits closer to true to size than the Copa Mundial does, so this buyer should not carry the Mundial's full-size-down rule over. Try it in 10 and 10.5. A key local constraint: **kangaroo-leather boots (Copa Mundial, Nike Premier 3, most Mizuno Morelia, NB 442 Pro Japan) cannot legally be sold in California**, so Berkeley stores should not stock them.

### Inferences
- The closest like-for-like replacement for the buyer's Legend 10 Elite is a current Tiempo, because the sizing carries over (TTS). For a buyer who liked the Legend 10's width, the Ligera Pro is probably the safer fit bet, since reviewers describe it as wider and more adjustable. The Maestro Elite is described as snugger through the midfoot, with a heel that one reviewer found too wide.
- For adidas, the Copa Mundial's full-size-down rule is specific to the Mundial. Its successors (Copa Pure 3/4, Copa Icon 2) are reported as true to size to half a size down. For this buyer that means 10-10.5, not 9.5.
- Because of the California kangaroo ban, a Berkeley shopper doing a same-day "play then maybe return" trial at local stores will realistically be choosing among synthetic or calf-leather models: Tiempo Maestro/Ligera, Copa Pure IV, Copa Icon 2, Copa Gloro 2, and Puma King 20.

### Gaps

- I found no source that directly compares Copa Pure IV sizing to the Tiempo Legend 10 or the Copa Mundial.

## Current Nike Tiempo lineup in 2026: names, tiers, materials, sizing vs Legend 10; Legend 10 Academy/Pro vs Elite

### Takeaway
Nike ended the Tiempo Legend line. The new Tiempo Maestro and Tiempo Ligera went on sale on January 22, 2026. The Maestro Elite uses a new synthetic "TECHLEATHER" and a Maestro360 split plate. The Ligera Pro uses TECHLEATHER on a traditional full-length plate. The Maestro Academy uses cheaper FlyTouch synthetic. Reviewers say both new models fit true to size in length. The Maestro is snugger through the midfoot, and the Ligera is wider and more accommodating.

- If the Legend 10 Elite fits at 10.5, try the Maestro Elite, Maestro Academy and Ligera Pro at 10.5 first. Try the Ligera in 11 only if 10.5 is tight across the width. Lockhart's advice argues against going down to 10 in the Maestro.
- The buyer describes the Legend 10 Elite as a perfect fit. Given the Maestro is snugger through the midfoot and has a wider, less secure heel, the Ligera Pro may feel closer to the Legend 10's comfort. That is a hypothesis to test in store.
- Big-box stores most often stock the Academy tier. The Maestro Academy is reportedly a good takedown, but its upper is thicker, less soft FlyTouch rather than TECHLEATHER. It will not reproduce the Elite's touch.

## Nike Premier 3 (kangaroo leather): still sold in 2026? Sizing?

### Takeaway

## FG vs AG vs FG/MG soleplates: which to pick for grass vs artificial turf

### Takeaway
Use FG on natural grass. On 3G/4G artificial grass, AG (more, shorter, often hollow studs) is safer and more durable. Nike Academy boots come as FG/MG hybrids. Puma sells the King 20 as FG/AG, and the Copa Icon 2 is also sold FG/AG. The Copa Mundial is FG-only (plus TF versions). Some brands can void the warranty if an FG boot is used on turf.

### Cited Findings
- FG studs are longer (conical or bladed) for natural firm grass. AG uses more, shorter, rounder, often hollow studs for 3G/4G long-pile turf. — [Pro:Direct US: ground types guide](https://www.prodirectsport.us/blogs/soccerblog/the-complete-guide-to-soccer-cleat-ground-types); [soccer.com: FG vs AG](https://www.soccer.com/guide/fg-vs-ag-soccer-cleats)
- On turf, FG studs "can catch and raise injury risk, and the abrasive surface wears the soleplate much faster, sometimes peeling it from the upper". Using the wrong cleat type "can affect warranty cover depending on the brand". — [Best Buy Soccer: FG, AG & SG explained](https://www.bestbuysoccer.com/blogs/buyers-guides/fg-vs-ag-vs-sg-cleats-which-soccer-cleat-studs-for-which-surface); [DMK Sports: FG vs AG](https://www.dmksports.com/blogs/sports/fg-vs-ag-cleats-is-fg-or-ag-better-for-your-game)
- Soleplate availability for the relevant models:
  - Maestro Elite: FG and AG-PRO. Ligera Pro: FG and AG. — [Unisport: Maestro Elite AG-PRO](https://www.unisportstore.com/football-boots/nike-tiempo-maestro-elite-ag-pro-shadow-blackice-blue/437997/); [SR4U: Ligera Pro AG](https://soccerreviewsforyou.com/2026/02/nike-tiempo-ligera-pro-ag-attack-pack-review/)
  - Maestro Academy: MG (multi-ground) and AG. — [Nike.com Maestro Academy AG](https://www.nike.com/t/tiempo-maestro-academy-artificial-grass-low-top-soccer-cleats-EQe0YHIU)

  - Copa Icon 2: FG/AG. — [Lockhart: Copa Icon II](https://lockhartbootblog.com/2025/04/01/adidas-copa-icon-ii-review/)
  - Copa Pure IV: FG and a League 2G/3G AG. — [Unisport](https://www.unisportstore.com/football-boots/copa-pure-iv-league-zeromtcblacklucred/431002/)

- The buyer's Copa Mundial (FG) is best kept for grass.

## Break-in: which need break-in vs comfortable out of the box (relevant to a same-day game and a possible return)

### Takeaway
The softest out of the box, per reviews: Nike Premier 3 (kangaroo), Copa Icon 2 (calf), Puma King 20 / King Ultimate (synthetic), and the Tiempo synthetics, which settle within one or two sessions. Needing some break-in: Copa Pure IV Elite (about 30-45 minutes reported) and the Ligera Pro (collar and heel, 3-5 wears per an aggregator). All leathers stretch, which matters when choosing a size, because a boot that feels perfect on day one may loosen.

### Cited Findings
- Premier 3: "feel as good as they ever will straight out of the box"; the leather stretches over time. — [Soccer Cleats 101: Premier III](https://www.soccercleats101.com/2024/01/06/nike-premier-iii-fg-a-deep-dive-review/)
- Legend 10 FlyTouch Plus: "contours to the foot within one to two sessions, offering immediate comfort similar to leather". — [BootHype: Legend 10 review](https://boothype.com/2023/10/07/nike-tiempo-legend-10-review-nike-ctr-in-disguise/); [Soccer Cleats 101](https://www.soccercleats101.com/2023/12/12/nike-tiempo-legend-10-elite-what-to-know-before-you-buy/)
- Maestro TECHLEATHER: "Tech Leather would not be so exciting if it only broke in quickly". The snippet is ambiguous; it implies the upper keeps softening over time. — [footballboots.co.uk](https://www.footballboots.co.uk/review-tiempo.html) (ambiguous snippet)
- Ligera Pro: "moderate break-in period of 3-5 wears… slight stiffness around the collar and heel". — [ShoesTrace (aggregator)](https://shoestrace.com/nike/tiempo-ligera-pro). Lockhart says the initial toe space is barely noticeable after warm-up. — [Lockhart: Ligera Pro](https://lockhartbootblog.com/2026/03/04/nike-tiempo-ligera-pro-review/)
- Copa Pure IV Elite: "needs 30 to 45 minutes to break in before it settles". — [Cleats Hub](https://cleatshub.com/review/adidas-copa-pure-4-elite)
- Copa Icon 2: "surprisingly soft out of the box"; it stretches "a bit more than expected". — [Lockhart: Copa Icon II](https://lockhartbootblog.com/2025/04/01/adidas-copa-icon-ii-review/); [Soccer Rehab](https://soccerrehab.com/blog/adidas-copa-icon-2-review)
- Puma King Ultimate (2023, K-BETTER): "soft, pliable and comfortable pretty much out of the box". The King 20 is described as "calmer", and its TOTALTouch+ as "soft, slightly cushioned". — [Soccer Cleats 101: King Ultimate](https://www.soccercleats101.com/2023/07/25/puma-king-ultimate-fg-complete-boot-review/); [footballboots.co.uk: King 20](https://www.footballboots.co.uk/puma-king.html)

- Playing a game in new boots the same day is lowest-risk with synthetic Tiempos, the King 20, or the Copa Icon 2. The Copa Pure IV Elite and Ligera Pro may feel stiff in the first hour.
- Wearing boots in a game usually makes them non-returnable under standard retail policies (cleats with turf or grass wear). That is a separate research topic and was not verified here.

### Gaps
- No lab or quantitative break-in data; everything here is reviewer impressions.

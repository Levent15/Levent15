Method note for the report writer: direct page fetches of Yelp, Google Maps, soccerpost.com, Berkeleyside, Instagram, Facebook and most directory sites were blocked from this research environment. ## 1. Which soccer specialty shops currently operate (2026)? Closures?

### Takeaway
The closest specialty shop is **Soccer Post El Cerrito (10351 San Pablo Ave)**. It is the old Berkeley "Soccer Pro" store on 10th St, which Soccer Post bought and then moved to El Cerrito in about January 2025. **The Berkeley 10th St store is closed.** Other shops that look like they are operating, in rough order of distance: East Bay Soccer, Chivamex and Soccer Mania (Oakland Fruitvale/International Blvd), Soccer Post Alameda, Leon Soccer Shop (San Leandro), Soccer Post Pleasant Hill, Elite Sports and SF Soccer (SF Mission), Solo Soccer Shop (South SF) and Soccer Post Dublin (at the edge of the 40-minute range). Soccer & Sport Shop in Walnut Creek is closed.

## 4. Return/exchange policy — especially cleats worn for one game

### Takeaway
None of these shops has a posted in-store policy that allows returning or exchanging worn cleats. Soccer Post's published policy (online orders) excludes "used, worn, or damaged items" from both returns and exchanges. Store-level practice at the franchise stores is not published. The buyer should assume **worn cleats will not be accepted** unless the store confirms otherwise by phone. No policy was found for any of the independent shops.

### Cited Findings
- Soccer Post's published policy: returns accepted within 30 days of delivery for most new, non-customized, **unused** items in original condition with tags and packaging; **"Used, worn, or damaged items cannot be returned or exchanged"**; customized items are final sale — [Soccer Post Returns & Exchanges](https://soccerpost.com/pages/returns-and-exchanges); [Soccer Post Refund Policy](https://soccerpost.com/policies/refund-policy).
- The same policy says retail stores cannot accept returns or exchanges of online or team orders. Snippets differ on whether this means "order returns" in general or "team order returns" specifically — [Soccer Post Returns & Exchanges](https://soccerpost.com/pages/returns-and-exchanges).
- Review evidence from Soccer Post Pleasant Hill: a parent bought $80 cleats, and after 20 minutes of practice the daughter had a large blister. The reviewer wrote "I tried to return...", but the snippet cuts off before the outcome — [Yahoo Local reviews](https://local.yahoo.com/info-21409679-soccer-pro-pleasant-hill/?stx=Soccer&csz=Antioch%2C+CA&fr=lsrp); [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/soccer-post-pleasant-hill).
- A search surfaced a "30-day return, unused/unwashed with tags, 50% restocking fee" policy for "Elite Sports," but it comes from **elitesports.com, an unrelated online retailer, not the SF Mission soccer shop**. Do not attribute it to Elite Sports SF — [elitesports.com policy](https://www.elitesports.com/pages/return-exchange-policy).
- A search summary said the old Soccer Pro Berkeley Yelp reviews include one mentioning concerns about the store's return policy, but no detail was visible — [Yelp Soccer Pro Berkeley](https://www.yelp.com/biz/soccer-pro-berkeley).

### Inferences
- The Pleasant Hill reviewer posting a complaint about attempting a return after one practice suggests (not confirmed) that the store did not accept the lightly worn cleats as a straight return.
- Since Soccer Post stores are locally operated franchises, a manager may allow a one-time exchange for fit problems. Nothing documents this, so the buyer should ask directly: "Will you exchange cleats worn once on grass if they don't fit?"

### Gaps
- There is no posted return or exchange policy for East Bay Soccer, Chivamex, Soccer Mania, Leon Soccer Shop, Elite Sports (SF), SF Soccer or Solo Soccer Shop. For each: **not posted; call to confirm.**
- I found no reviews (positive or negative) describing a successful return or exchange of worn cleats at any of these shops.

## 6. Fitting help / service reputation

### Takeaway
Elite Sports (SF) has the strongest service reputation: bilingual, knowledgeable staff focused on footwear fit. Soccer Post Alameda is described as friendly and helpful. Chivamex has mixed service reviews.

### Cited Findings
- Elite Sports: "super helpful, bilingual staff". Reviewers commend "knowledgeable and enthusiastic staff … dedicated to … assisting in finding the right fit, especially when it comes to footwear" — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/elite-sports-san-francisco).
- Soccer Post Alameda: described as "very friendly and extremely helpful," with "a decent selection of soccer field, turf and indoor shoes" — [Yelp East Bay soccer store search](https://www.yelp.com/search?find_desc=Soccer+Store&find_loc=East+Bay,+CA) / [Yelp Alameda](https://www.yelp.com/biz/soccer-post-alameda-alameda).
- Leon Soccer Shop: reviews mention "great quality soccer gear, friendly and nice staff" — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/leon-soccer-shop-san-leandro).
- SF Soccer: called "the best soccer store in San Francisco with very friendly staff and best prices" — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/sf-soccer-san-francisco).
- Chivamex: 2.5 stars on 6 Yelp reviews, with complaints about service and product durability — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/chivamex-sports-soccer-oakland).
- Old Soccer Pro Berkeley (now closed or moved): 4 stars, with praise for competitive prices and authentic kits — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/soccer-pro-berkeley).
- The Willow Soccer (Menlo Park, out of range): praised for a knowledgeable staff member who helped choose cleats — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/the-willow-soccer-menlo-park-2).

### Inferences
- For an adult buyer who wants help fitting a leather boot and a good chance of premium stock, the practical Saturday plan is Soccer Post El Cerrito first (closest, long Saturday hours if the 9–8 listing is right), then Elite Sports in SF's Mission (the best-reviewed independent). Soccer Post Alameda or Pleasant Hill are backups for sizes El Cerrito doesn't have.

### Gaps
- There are no reviews specific to Soccer Post El Cerrito's service since the 2025 move (Yelp not accessible), and no review count for that page was captured.
